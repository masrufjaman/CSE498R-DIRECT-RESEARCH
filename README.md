# CSE498R-DIRECT-RESEARCH

Directed research courses allow undergraduate students to pursue areas of inquiry and topics of interest that are not generally offered through approved curriculum courses, under the supervision of a faculty member.

In this course, students design and carry out an independent project while working one-on-one with a faculty supervisor. The course expects a high level of self-directed learning and requires students to:

- read and conduct research independently,
- complete written examinations, reports, projects, research papers, portfolios, or similar assignments designed to measure competency in the stated objectives,
- engage in experiential work, directed reading, or independent research supervised by a faculty member.
