## Our Research Interests
Our primary research interest is to ***solve problems of the modern societies and improve lifestyles of the people***. Usually, it requires a concerted effort from the government organizations to pinpoint the real causes of the problems and finding timely solutions. We are working to find novel usages of internet edge devices (mobile devices, IoT sensors etc.) to identify societal problems, then find novel ways to interpret the problems and find solutions using statistics, machine learning, and big data algorithms.
